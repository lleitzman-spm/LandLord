# Opening slice: execution plan

User-approved mandate, 2 November 2026: Unity first subject to a bounded toolchain check, a coherent first 20 to 30 minutes, room to explore before commitments create pressure. `DEVELOPMENT-BRIEF.md` records the agreed game direction and takes precedence over inherited contradictory constraints. This plan describes intended delivery, not completed features.

## Target experience

A new player chooses a neighbourhood, investigates competing properties, buys, manages an existing or new tenancy, commissions scoped work, collects rent, considers an institutional opportunity and makes a second investment decision. There should be several defensible starting strategies. Success is understanding why a decision mattered and wanting to try the next deal.

The clock can be paused for reading and planning. The opening has no compulsory emergency or arbitrary failure deadline. Money and time become constrained by purchases, leases and work the player actually chooses. Expensive commitments can still cause real distress. Balance exploration against consequences; do not remove the economic trade-offs.

### 2. Implement the property loop in one integrated project

- Seeded starting market and neighbourhood choice; coherent grant/purchase/repair/rent arithmetic with a liquidity buffer.
- Authored offers with occupied and vacant options. Buying updates ownership, cash, leases and market availability consistently.
- Fixed underlying property facts, dated evidence and a searchable compact history. Repeated inspection does not reroll or duplicate charges for the same completed report.
- Personal investigation consumes time; professional work consumes agreed money and time. Paid reports have scope and limits.
- Explicit instructions, scope and spending limits for the first repair. Discovering extra work does not authorize it. Missing instructions leave it undone, with readable consequences.
- Commercial occupants remain independent tenants. Their activity contributes a few comprehensible property effects, not inventories or production chains.
- One institutional property opportunity with competing interests and a practical benefit/cost. Optional historical clues stay available without forcing supernatural encounters.
- Rent, maintenance, occupancy and compact property effects feed the next investment choice. Distress can require selling down; the opening is not a forced bankruptcy lesson.
- Save and reload preserve actual facts, knowledge, ownership, leases, money, work, instructions and consequential agreements.

Review evidence: complete playable loop, a short recorded walkthrough, readable decision screens and relevant rules/integration tests. Include at least two different starting strategies; do not verify only a scripted winning path.

### 3. Make the same slice visually coherent and ready for hands-on review

- One small living place with readable occupant/staff movement and useful state feedback. Pick behaviour that helps explain the property loop rather than decorative complexity.
- Illustrated property/character views share architecture and identity with simpler real-time assets. Preserve entrances, rooflines, routes and structural relationships across views.
- Reuse recovered approved assets when actually available. Record provenance and any reconstruction; generated art is concept work until import, consistency and usage rights are checked for the intended use.
- Keep silhouettes, faces and views varied; avoid clay-like materials. All guards and soldiers are men.
- Clear mouse/keyboard controls, pause, legible text and unobstructed property interactions. No separately metered inference service is a runtime prerequisite.
- Build the agreed PC target, launch it, exercise the loop and verify save/reload in that build. A compiled project alone is not proof it plays.

Review evidence: versioned build/source, screenshot set, short clip, test results, known limitations and a concise playtest sheet. If platform access prevents a check, state which check remains unrun.

## Concurrent lanes

- **Engine and integration:** establish the toolchain, own shared state/interfaces, integrate and validate the playable path.
- **Rules and verification:** deterministic property facts, economy, scoped delegation, time, saves and meaningful tests. Begin engine-dependent implementation only after the engine gate is resolved.
- **Narrative and content:** concrete opening offers, records, professional dialogue and one institutional deal, with proposed balance data kept separate from confirmed canon.
- **Visuals and presentation:** asset recovery/inventory, coherent place studies and selected character assets. Editor-independent content can continue while toolchain prerequisites are blocked.

Assign distinct files/interfaces to agents. Root owns integration and approves contract changes. Use task-appropriate model/effort; delegate bounded tasks rather than creating idle agents to fill slots. Reports show actual work and evidence, not unseen candidates.

## Scope boundaries

The first milestone does not implement kingdom conquest, full faction simulation, multiple church systems, room-layout construction or player-run businesses. The design should leave sensible extension points without building speculative systems for each later feature. A future wardenship can be foreshadowed through a real lead; it does not require a complete territorial economy now.

New meter proposals must explain which repeated player decision they improve. Prefer existing property effects, authored projects and persistent consequential records to tracking minute physical details.

## Autonomy and review

Proceed independently on ordinary code, content, balance and visual iteration within the brief. Keep work moving across useful lanes; batch nonblocking creative questions with concrete candidates at reviews. Surface prerequisites, material scope changes and engine substitution decisions promptly. Do not treat unanswered required decisions as approval.

Review at three useful points: a verified engine/build path, the integrated property loop, and the playable/art pass. Screenshots, clips and playable artifacts should precede broad requests for feedback. The user need not approve each name, line, parameter or implementation detail.
