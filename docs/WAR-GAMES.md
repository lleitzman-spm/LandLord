# War Games — the master plan

*Named 2026-07-19 (Edwin). The realm's proving ground: a live simulation of the whole operation
where knights (property managers) train against every scenario before real combat — and where the
kingdom tests and hardens its own machine. Working names floated: War Games / Grand Melee / the
Tourney. **War Games** stands.*

## Two things at once

War Games is one build that serves two masters:

1. **The proving ground (for us).** A full simulation of the operating model — real seats, a real
   portfolio's worth of doors, work "boxes" flowing through the operator (catalog → flow engine →
   the hands) with an advanceable clock. We pour realistic fuel in at volume and *watch whether it
   moves through correctly*: queues fill and drain, aging climbs, cascades run to done or stall,
   breaches surface. This is how the operator is tested and hardened without touching a single real
   client record (the data gate stays shut — see "The fuel" below).

2. **The product (for the market).** The same environment, turned outward, is a **training video
   game for property managers**: scenario after scenario — the move-out that goes sideways, the
   delinquency that spirals, the renewal wave, the owner who won't fund the reserve — run against a
   living instrument that scores the play. A PM levels up by *doing the job in the melee* before
   they ever hold a real door.

The two are the same engine. That is the point.

## The strategic thesis (Edwin, 2026-07-19)

**The market's misread — the Jevons trap.** The whole industry is pricing the *human* property
manager toward irrelevance: "AI is coming, why invest in training a person who's about to be
obsolete?" So nobody funds the human. This is exactly backwards. Jevons paradox: when a capability
gets radically cheaper, demand for it does not fall — it *explodes*. The cheaper it is to make a
great PM, the more great-PM-work the market will buy.

**What we prove — two curves crossing.** We do not merely predict the shift; War Games *proves* it,
live and measurable:

- the **cost to train** a property manager is falling through the floor (a game trains for cents
  what a year of shadowing costs), while
- the **capability** of a trained PM is *compounding* — because the AI *augments* the human (the
  clerk model: agents do the mechanical work, the human keeps judgment), it does not delete them.

A dirt-cheap-to-train, AI-amplified property manager is a superhuman operator. The market thinks
that role is dying; we're building the machine that makes it the most leveraged role there is.

**The window — the arbitrage.** Until AI fully dominates the market on its own (a shift that could
take ~10 years), the delta is ours: **training cost in the dirt, revenue potential enormous.** We
own the decade in between. The training game is the wedge that opens it.

## The first War Game — a firm, anonymized

*Ratified direction 2026-07-19.* Make the first game the very thing we are preparing for: **move
a firm's ~200-door portfolio into the system.**

- **Exactly our people.** The real seats and hands — Alys, Mabel, Osric, and the rest of the
  census as it stands — run the game. The org is real; only the doors and tenants are invented.
- **Anonymized, synthesized data.** No real client content crosses the walls (the data gate holds).
  Instead we *generate* a realistic portfolio from the operation's shape: a few hundred doors,
  owners, tenants, leases — randomized, plausible, and **fun**: medieval-flavored names (Bob the
  Tanner, Agnes the Alewife, the Widow of Willow Row) so the melee reads as the kingdom it is.
- **The boxes.** A stream of work flows in — move-outs, work orders, delinquencies, renewals, rent
  postings — routed through the catalog and the flow engine across the seats, at realistic cadence
  and volume.
- **The clock.** The readings already take `now` as a parameter (pure, clock injected), so a
  **simulated clock you can advance** is the natural spine: tick a day or a week forward and watch
  the boxes move. That is how "does it move through correctly?" gets answered.

Factory-clean, working fluid, gate-safe: LandLord builds the mechanism; a firm's *shape* (not its real
records) is one loaded game. When the data gate opens later, real doors pour through the same slots.
