# Brief: sim-world

**Your files:** `src/sim/world.ts`, `src/sim/path.ts`, `src/sim/buildings.ts`, `src/sim/actors.ts`, `src/sim/jobs.ts`, `test/sim-world.test.ts`.
**Read:** `src/sim/types.ts`, `core.ts`, `index.ts`, `src/data/schema.ts`, `ids.ts`, `buildings.ts`, `balance.ts`, `ranks.ts`, and the other `src/sim/*.ts` files for the functions you call.

Replace the placeholder bodies with the real thing. ARCHITECTURE.md ("Sim: how the pieces interlock") defines your behaviour; details:

**Map.** `createMap` from `s.seed`: mostly grass; a stream of `water` wandering north to south through the eastern third; forest clumps, denser toward the edges; scattered rock; a few `field` tiles. Parcel 5 (tiles 8..15, 8..15) mostly clear and owned. A `road` from the west edge gate (`gatePoint`) into parcel 5, with a `bridge` wherever a road crosses water. Twelve parcels with comic names, prices rising with distance from parcel 5, `minRank` by ring (neighbours of 5: reeve; next ring: knight; far corners: baron). `buy_parcel` checks adjacency to an owned parcel, `rank.maxParcels`, rank, gold (`land`).

**Placement.** `canPlace`: bounds, every tile owned, terrain grass/field/road only (a road under a building is removed), no overlap, `rank.isUnlocked`, staff buildings and the seat are one-of-a-kind, and enough gold (refuse `no_gold` in both modes). `place_building` charges the cost (`construction`), creates the building in state `building`, and queues a `build` job (role `mason`, `workDays = def.buildDays`). When it completes: `ready`, condition 100, units created and listed, `building_done`, a herald line. `demolish` (no tenants inside; a short job), `rename_building`, `set_listed`, `place_road` / `remove_road` (cheap, instant, 1 crown a tile), `clear_tile` (forest or rock; a `clear_land` job with a small cost). `upgrade_building` is refused (`locked`) except through `upgradeSeat`.

**Decay.** Monthly: `def.decay` times modifiers (vacant 1.5, winter 1.2, squatters 2, occupant `wear` traits). Emit `building_changed` when condition crosses 75 / 50 / 25. At 0 the building is `ruined`: households leave (`tenants.moveOut(…, 'ruin')`), it can only be `renovate`d. `order_job` for `repair` (restores up to +35; cost and days scale with what is missing) and `renovate` (to 100; 40% of build cost). Lower condition raises the monthly chance of a maintenance incident: call `incidents.spawnDecision(s, 'repair', { buildingId })` (about 4% a month at 100, 30% at 30; never while one is already open there).

**Desirability.** 50 base, plus auras of ready buildings within radius (linear falloff), +4 for an adjacent road, plus (condition − 60) / 4, minus nuisances (squatters or a ruin within 4 tiles, nasty visuals such as fire or rats nearby). Clamp 0..100. Cache per whole day.

**Actors.** The ruler's figure exists from init at the seat's entrance. Staff get an actor when hired (staff.ts calls `spawnActor`; you home them at their staff building's entrance each day if it exists). Movement along A* paths (8-neighbour, no corner cutting, roads cost less; `BAL.walkSpeed`, `BAL.roadSpeedBonus`), `facing` updated, tile `wear` nudged up as they walk (and decaying slowly everywhere). Idle actors drift home. `go_to` walks the ruler and cancels nothing.

**Jobs.** Exactly as ARCHITECTURE describes, including: at Reeve rank the ruler does every job; a staff-role job with no such delegate falls to the ruler; `'any'` prefers the matching delegate. One job per actor at a time; the ruler's queue honours `priority` then age, and `byRuler` jobs jump the queue. Work time divides by delegate skill (1 + (skill−1)×0.2) and temperament `speed`; the ruler works at 1.0. Cost through `economy.spend` at work start (category from the job kind). Emit `job` phases and call `core.notifyJobDone` once. `collect_purse` / `collect_all` queue `collect` jobs (role `bailiff`, about 0.1 day of work each), deduplicated. `order_job` validates the target and creates the right job for kinds `repair`, `renovate`, `demolish`, `clear_land`, `show`, `screen`, `serve_notice`, `evict`, `inspect`, `visit`. Jobs whose target vanished are cancelled.

**Names.** `makeBuildingName`: a large, funny, type-aware generator ("The Leaning Hovel", "Damp End", "Mudlark Cottage", "Three-Stack"), no repeats in a realm.

**Tests.** Node tests (`npx tsx test/sim-world.test.ts`, plain asserts): determinism from a seed; placement refusals; a build job walked and finished by the ruler; pathing around water over the bridge; decay bands; job priority; 500 simulated days without a throw or NaN. Run a full `Sim.create()` and advance it; other modules are being rewritten around you, so failures inside their files are not yours, but nothing of yours may throw.
