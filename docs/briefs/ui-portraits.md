# Brief: ui-portraits (code-drawn faces that move)

**Your files:** `src/ui/portraits.ts` and anything under `src/ui/portraits/`, `src/dev/gallery-portraits.ts`.
**Read:** the `PortraitSpec` / `Portrait` shapes already in `src/ui/portraits.ts` (keep them), `src/word/api.ts` (Mood), `src/ui/css/base.ts` (tokens), `src/data/ids.ts` (SPECIES, TRADES, NPCS), `src/data/schema.ts` (the `look` hints on CharacterDef and NpcDef), `docs/STYLE.md` on species.

The owner turned down generated images in favour of faces drawn in code. So these carry every conversation in the game. Build a parametric portrait system in SVG: **ink line and flat wash in the manner of a manuscript miniature**, head and shoulders in a roundel (lapis or deep red ground, a thin gold ring, optional), assembled from parts so there are effectively unlimited distinct people in one consistent hand.

- **Parts**, all driven deterministically by `seed`: head shape, jaw, nose (many), eyes (shape, spacing, colour), brows, mouth, ears, hair (many styles and colours, greying), facial hair, wrinkles and freckles and scars, neck and shoulders, clothing with collar types, and headwear. A slightly wobbly hand-inked line (not geometric perfection), two-tone wash shading, a rosy cheek. Faces should have *character*: big noses, weak chins, magnificent brows.
- **Species**: human; halfling (round, rosy, curly, small); dwarf (broad, a serious beard on everyone, braids, heavy brows); goblin (green, huge ears, wide toothy mouth, bright eyes; officious, never grotesque); elf (long, fine, pointed ears, half-lidded calm); ogre (wide grey-green head, small features, underbite, kind eyes, a hat too small).
- **Trades and roles**: a hat, hood, coif or tool-at-the-shoulder for each of the 28 trades (flour on the baker, soot on the smith, a pointed hat on the wizard); the four delegates' liveries; the ruler by rank (circlet, coronet, crown); and a fixed, memorable look for each of the fifteen NPC ids (the crown herald with a tabard and trumpet, the moneylender with tiny spectacles and a patient smile, the dragon adjuster who is in fact a small dragon with a ledger, `your_mother`...). Honour `look` hints (hair, beard, hat, feature) when given.
- **Life**: blink at natural random intervals; pupils that wander or follow `lookAt`; `setMood` reshapes brows, eyelids, mouth and cheeks across all nine moods so each is readable at a glance; `setTalking` flaps the mouth through a few shapes; a slow breathing bob. Animate with CSS or a single shared rAF, pausing when off-screen or detached; `dispose` cleans up. Respect `prefers-reduced-motion`.
- **Sizes**: must read at 40px (lists: simplify detail automatically below about 64px) and reward a look at 360px (audiences).

**Dev gallery.** `src/dev/gallery-portraits.ts`: a parchment page with a grid: each species × many seeds; every trade hat; all NPCs; one face in all nine moods; the same face at 40, 96 and 360px. Screenshot (use `--shots` to catch blinks and talking), look, and iterate until they are funny, warm and unmistakably one family. No two neighbours in the grid should look alike.
