# Brief: render-core

**Your files:** `src/render/index.ts`, everything you create under `src/render/core/`, `src/dev/render-core.ts`.
**Read:** `src/render/api.ts` (you implement `Renderer`), `palette.ts`, `buildings/types.ts`, `townlife.ts` and `actors.ts` (the `FigureLayer` / `LayerHelpers` shapes), the placeholder `buildings/index.ts`, `figures/index.ts`, `visuals/index.ts`, `fx.ts` (other agents are replacing these; call them only through the api.ts shapes), `src/sim/types.ts`, `core.ts`, `src/data/buildings.ts`, `src/dev/fixture.ts`.
**Reference only (do not copy wholesale, do not import):** `/home/claude/landlord-old/src/table/WarTableFrame.tsx` and `WarTableView.tsx` are an earlier candle-lit war table the owner liked. Skim them for what made the table feel right, then do better.

You build the stage everyone else performs on: `createRenderer(deps)`.

**Scene.** A dark room. A large oak table (procedural canvas wood grain, darker edges, iron studs). On it, the realm **board**: a raised slab the size of the map plus a bevelled wooden frame, its top the terrain. Around the board, real props that mean things and can be picked (`PickResult.prop`): an open **coffer** whose coin pile grows with `state.gold` (log scale; empty and cobwebbed in debt), the **ledger** book, the **charter** scroll with a wax seal, an inkwell and quill, a bell, scattered coins, and three or four **candles** in brass holders with flickering flames. The candles burn down across the month and are fresh on the 1st.

**Terrain.** Tile ground with vertex colours: season-blended grass with gentle variation, dirt roads, bridges, water with a slow shimmer, rocks, `field` furrows, and forest as instanced low-poly trees (two or three shapes; sway with `env.wind`; leaf colours by season; bare and snow-capped in winter). Unowned parcels are dimmer and slightly desaturated with a rope-and-stake boundary and a small signpost; owned land is bright. Tile `wear` darkens the grass into desire paths. `env.snow` whitens everything. Rebuild only what changed (`tiles_changed`, `parcel_bought`).

**Light and the month.** Compute `Env` every frame from the state (`core.dateOf`): the month is the table's day. Dawn on the 1st (low, rosy-gold, long shadows), a long warm afternoon, orange dusk around phase 0.75, a short blue night (roughly 0.86 to 0.96: `env.night` → 1, candles and windows carry the scene), then dawn. One shadow-casting warm key light fitted to the board, a cool fill, hemisphere ambient, and candle point lights (no shadows). ACES tone mapping, sRGB, soft shadows. Seasons tint the light; snow raises `env.snow` in winter and melts in spring.

**Post.** Render to a target and finish with one pass: tilt-shift (blur growing toward the top and bottom of the frame, sharp across a band around the camera target), a gentle bloom on flames and lit windows, vignette, a touch of grain, a warm grade. `quality: 'low'` skips post and halves shadow resolution. The game must hold 60 fps on a laptop integrated GPU with 60 buildings and 100 figures.

**Camera.** Orbit around a target on the board: wheel or pinch zooms toward the cursor, right-drag or two-finger drag rotates, left-drag pans (tool `select`), WASD and arrows pan, Q/E rotate. Damped, clamped pitch (about 25° to 70°), clamped zoom and target. `focus()` tweens. Modes: `title` is a slow close orbit with no input; `audience` eases low and close on the seat with stronger blur; `ceremony` a slow push on the seat.

**Reconcile.** `reset` / `sync`: one `BuildingVisual` per building via `createBuildingVisual(look, ctx)`; derive the `BuildingLook` from state and call `set` only when it changed. Place by footprint centre, rotate by `-rot * PI / 2`. Pop new buildings in with a bounce and `fx.puff`; drop removed ones in dust. Attach `createVisual` handles for each id in `building.visuals` (call `leave` when one goes). Selection ring and hover highlight. Create and drive the two figure layers (`createActorLayer`, `createTownLife`) and `createFx`. In `handleEvents`: positive `gold` events with a `buildingId` send `fx.coins` from that roof to the coffer; `purse_collected` likewise; `weather` → `fx.setWeather`; `building_done` → confetti of dust; `promotion` → `fx.confetti` over the seat.

**Tools and picking.** `place`: a translucent ghost of the real building (green when `deps.canPlace`, red otherwise) snapped to the tile under the cursor, footprint tiles shown; click emits `place`. `road` / `unroad`: drag to paint, emit `paint` once per stroke. `clear`, `demolish`: hover highlight, click emits. Right-click or Esc emits `cancel`. `pick` order: figures (layers), buildings, table props, ground tile. Distinguish a click from a drag. `anchor(ref)` projects to CSS pixels (building top, door, actor head, tenant head via the town layer, tile, prop). `setHighlight` tints tiles (parcels for sale).

**Dev entry.** `src/dev/render-core.ts` builds the renderer on `makeFixtureState('knight')`, runs the loop, and exposes `window.__dev = { setDay(d), setMode(m), focus(...), state }` for `--eval`. Screenshot dawn, afternoon, dusk and night; winter; the title orbit; a close zoom. Look at every shot and iterate until it is beautiful with the placeholder buildings, because the real ones will drop in.
