**How to write one.** Start from a real thing that happens to people who manage rental property, find its medieval costume, and play it dead straight. Give the petitioner a reasonable point. Make every outcome defensible, with honest trade-offs in the effects (coin against happiness against renown against condition against time). Effects must be modest and proportionate to the rank: at Reeve a crown matters (rents are 8 to 16); at Baron tens of crowns. Typical magnitudes: happiness ±3 to ±15, renown ±1 to ±3, favor ±2 to ±6, condition ±4 to ±20. `intent` is a plain sentence for a model deciding which outcome the ruler meant. `say` is what a ruler might actually say aloud. `reply` and `result` carry the comedy. Roughly a third should be audiences (`audience: true`, with 2 or 3 `opening` lines and a `stance`); the rest are map pins with exactly one `default: true` outcome and usually a `job`. Use `requires` so an incident only appears where it makes sense, `seasons` where apt, `minRank` to spread them (about 45% reeve, 35% knight, 20% baron), `cooldownMonths` of 8 to 20, and `weight` (0.3 rare and special, 1 ordinary). Give visuals to the ones that deserve to be seen. Chains are welcome: a `spawn` effect to another incident **in your own file** (set that follow-up incident to `weight: 0` so only the chain spawns it). Vary structure and length; do not let them fall into one rhythm.

**Check before you finish:** `node tools/check.mjs src/data/incidents` and `npx tsx tools/validate-content.ts` (it lints ids, placeholders, lengths, dashes, option tags; errors about incidents in other files are not yours, but every error naming one of your ids must be gone).
