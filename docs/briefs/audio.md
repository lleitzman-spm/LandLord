# Brief: audio (everything you hear, synthesised)

**Your files:** `src/audio/index.ts` and anything under `src/audio/`, `src/dev/audio-check.ts`.
**Read:** `src/audio/api.ts` (you implement `createAudio`), `src/data/ids.ts` (SFX: all of them), `src/sim/types.ts` (SimEvent, GameState), `core.ts` (`dateOf`).

No audio files: WebAudio only. It must sound warm and wooden, like a small consort playing in the corner of a candle-lit room, never like a synth demo or a beeping app.

- **Engine**: one `AudioContext` created lazily on `unlock()` (called from the first user gesture), master → compressor, separate gains for music, sfx and voice, a simple convolution or feedback reverb for the room. Everything is a no-op before unlock and when the context is unavailable. `setVolumes` ramps smoothly.
- **SFX**: every `SfxId`, each a small designed sound from oscillators, noise and envelopes: `coin` (a bright double clink), `coins` (a pour), `hammer`, `saw`, `bell` (a real-sounding struck bell with inharmonic partials), `horn` (a herald's short call), `door`, `knock`, `page`, `quill` (scratch), `stamp` (the wax seal thump), `thud`, `pop`, `whoosh`, `error` (a low wooden clunk, never a buzzer), `success`, `fanfare` (three or four notes, brassy), `growl`, `squawk` (the griffin), `squeak`, `splash`, `fire`, `thunder`, `gavel`, `cheer` (a small crowd made of filtered noise bursts and vowels), `boo`, `pluck`, `chime`, `click`, `build_done`, `herald`, `place`, `crumble`, `magic`, `sigh`, `laugh`. Slight random pitch and timing so repeats do not machine-gun; a voice limit; `pan` support.
- **Events** (`handleEvents`): rent coins on `rent_paid` / `purse_collected` (rate-limited so twenty at once becomes a pleasing shower), the bell on `rent_day`, the horn on `crown` and `urgent` heralds and a soft chime on others, hammering while build and repair jobs run, `build_done`, `stamp` on incident resolved, `gavel` on court matters, `fanfare` on promotion, `squawk` when a griffin visual appears, weather beds for rain, storm (with `thunder`) and wind in winter, `cheer` on a feast, `crumble` on ruin.
- **Music**: a generative medieval ambience: a plucked lute or harp voice (Karplus-Strong), a soft recorder or flute line, a drone and a frame-drum tap, playing modal phrases (Dorian, Mixolydian) from a small phrase grammar so it never loops obviously and never resolves cheesily. Moods (`setMood`, cross-faded): `title` (spacious, one instrument), `calm`, `busy`, `rent_day` (brighter, a little dance), `tense`, `court` (sparse, attentive), `winter` (slow, high drone), `triumph`, `defeat`, `off`. `update(state, dt)` chooses a mood from the state (the first three days of the month: `rent_day`; many urgent incidents or debt: `tense`; winter; otherwise by how busy the realm is) unless a mood was set explicitly within the last few seconds. Tempo relaxes when paused. Quiet by default: it sits under everything.
- **Voice** (`voice(seed, text, opts)`): speech blips for dialogue: a run of short pitched syllables whose count and rhythm follow the text (pauses at punctuation), with a formant-ish timbre and pitch range chosen by `seed`, so the goblin chief sounds unlike the widow. Returns a stopper. Charming at low volume, never grating.

**Check.** You cannot hear it here, so verify structurally: `src/dev/audio-check.ts` renders each sfx, each mood for ten seconds and a voice line through an `OfflineAudioContext` and reports per sound: duration, peak, RMS, DC offset and whether anything clipped or was silent or contained NaN; run it through `tools/shot.mjs` (the page prints to the console, which the tool echoes) and fix what it reveals (clipping, silence, runaway feedback). Make the offline renderer share the real synthesis code.
