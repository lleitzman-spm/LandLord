# Brief: word-audience (talking a petitioner through to a ruling) and the microphone

**Your files:** `src/word/audience.ts`, `audience-prompt.ts`, `mic.ts`, `test/word-audience.test.ts`, `tools/audience-harness.ts`.
**Read:** `src/word/api.ts` (AudienceSession, AudienceTurn, Speaker, Mood, MicAPI, ModelClient, WordDeps), the signatures in the placeholder `audience.ts` and `mic.ts` (keep them), `src/sim/types.ts` (Incident, Tenant, Applicant, Oath, OathKind, Action), `src/data/schema.ts` (IncidentDef, OutcomeDef, CharacterDef, NpcDef), `src/data/incidents/system.ts` and `examples.ts`, `src/data/index.ts`, `docs/BIBLE.md` "Ruling by word", `docs/STYLE.md`, `src/dev/fixture.ts`.

**`openAudienceSession(incidentId, deps, model, onChange)`** returns a live conversation about one open incident (null if it is not open). It builds the `Speaker` (the tenant, applicant, or NPC from the def's `petitioner`; mood from happiness), the first line (one of the def's `opening` lines, filled with the incident's `vars`, chosen stably by incident id), and the whispers (each outcome's `label` and `say`, filled).

**`say(text)`** sends one turn to the model and returns an `AudienceTurn`. The prompt (`audience-prompt.ts`) gives the model:
- who it is playing: name, species (with the species note from STYLE.md), trade, household, tags, revealed AND hidden traits (it may let hidden ones show in manner; if the ruler draws one out, it reports it in `revealed`), the named character's `bio` and `voice` when there is one, or the NPC's; their **memories of the ruler** (use them: grudges and gratitude should surface naturally); happiness; rent, arrears; how long they have lived there;
- the matter: the incident's title, herald line, `stance`, and the outcomes as a list of `{ id, intent }`;
- the ruler's rank and name, the season, and the realm's relevant policies;
- the conversation so far;
- the rules: stay in character and in the STYLE.md voice (dry, specific, under 200 characters, no em dashes, no modern words, never mention being a game or a model); argue your corner like a person, concede when treated fairly; **never invent numbers or outcomes**: the only things that can happen are the listed outcomes;
- the output, strict JSON only: `{ "reply": string, "mood": Mood, "outcome": id or null, "oaths": [{ "kind": OathKind, "text": string, "months": number, "amount"?: number }], "revealed": TraitId[] }`. `outcome` is set only when the ruler's words clearly amount to one of the listed rulings (match by `intent`); while the ruler is asking questions, haggling, or being vague, it stays null and the petitioner keeps talking (and may ask "So what is it to be, {title}?" after a few turns). `oaths` only when the ruler actually promised something concrete in this turn.
When an outcome comes back: validate it, dispatch `resolve_incident`, then dispatch `swear_oath` for each valid oath (fill `toTenantId`, `toName`, `buildingId`, `dueDay`), `reveal_trait` for genuinely hidden traits the household has, and a `remember` with a one-line memory of how the audience went (valence from the outcome and mood). Fill `resultText` from the outcome's `result`. If the sim refuses, the petitioner says so in character and the session stays open. Cap a session at ten ruler turns (then the Steward's whisper insists on a ruling).
**`choose(outcomeId)`** rules directly (whisper click offline, or the cap): the reply is the outcome's pre-written `reply`. **Offline** (`model().kind === 'none'` or a failed call): `say` does keyword matching of the ruler's text against each outcome's `label`, `say` and `intent` to pick a ruling when one clearly matches; otherwise the petitioner answers with a gentle in-character nudge toward the whispers. A transport error mid-session falls back to offline for that turn, never a dead end.
**`leave()`** closes without ruling.

**The microphone** (`mic.ts`): `createMic()` over the Web Speech API (`SpeechRecognition` / `webkitSpeechRecognition`): `supported` by feature detection; `start` with interim results streaming to `onPartial` and one `onFinal`; `stop()` ends and finalises; map errors (`not-allowed`, `service-not-allowed` → `denied`; `no-speech`; others); language from `navigator.language` falling back to `en-US`; safe against double starts; `listening` kept honest. No audio is recorded or stored by the game.

**Harness and tests.** `tools/audience-harness.ts <incidentDefId> "<what the ruler says>"` prints the exact prompt for a fixture household so other agents can try it against real models. `test/word-audience.test.ts`: a scripted `ModelClient` driving sessions for the griffin, late rent, goblins and the applicant: ruling on the first turn; two turns of haggling then a ruling; an oath extracted and sworn; a hostile reply (an outcome id that does not exist, an invented oath kind, 5,000 characters) handled; offline matching across the whispers of every incident in `INCIDENTS` (each outcome's own `say` line must select that outcome offline, or you report which do not).
