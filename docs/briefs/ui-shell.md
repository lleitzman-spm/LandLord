# Brief: ui-shell (the frame of the interface)

**Your files:** `src/ui/index.ts`, `src/ui/parts/hud.ts`, `heralds.ts`, `pins.ts`, `bubbles.ts`, `src/ui/css/shell.ts`, `src/dev/ui-shell.ts`.
**Read:** `src/ui/api.ts` (you implement `createUI`, `UI`, `UICtx`), `kit.ts`, `css/base.ts` (tokens and classes: use them), `parts/part.ts`, the placeholder parts and `screens/*.ts` (other agents are writing `buildmenu`, `inspector`, `wordbar` and every screen; you mount them by their fixed exports `mount(ctx)` / `create(ctx)`), `icons.ts`, `marginalia.ts`, `portraits.ts` (art agents are filling these; call them as documented), `src/render/api.ts` (Renderer: `anchor`, `on`, `focus`, `setSelection`), `src/word/api.ts`, `src/audio/api.ts`, `src/sim/types.ts`, `core.ts` (`fmtDate`, `dateOf`), `src/dev/fixture.ts`.

**`createUI`**: injects `BASE_CSS` and every part's CSS, builds `#ui` inside `deps.root`, creates the `UICtx`, mounts parts into named slots, and manages screens (one at a time; `ctx.open/close/isOpen`; Esc closes; screens with `pauses` hold the world at speed 0 and restore it; parts hide behind full screens and on the title screen, i.e. when `!app.inGame()`). Tracks selection: renderer `pick` events → `ctx.select` → `renderer.setSelection` and every part's `onSelect`. Picking a table prop opens its screen (ledger → `ledger`, charter → `charter`, coffer → `ledger`, bell → pause toggle). `ctx.act` dispatches and toasts refusals (`result.reason`). `ctx.holdCrawl()` sets speed 0.1 and returns a release that restores the previous speed (nesting-safe). Toasts and tooltips (`title` is not enough: a small parchment tooltip for elements with `data-tip`). Hotkeys: Space pause, 1/2/3 speeds, B build menu, L ledger, C council, H help, Enter or T focuses the Word box, Esc cancels tool then closes screens. Never steal keys while a text field has focus. Wire renderer tool events (`place`, `paint`, `clear`, `demolish`, `cancel`) to actions. `rebind(sim)` swaps the sim. Click and UI sounds through `audio.sfx`.

**Layout** (desktop; everything is compact so the table stays the star):
- top-left: the **HUD plaque**; top-right: the **heralds**; left edge under the plaque: slot `left` (the tool rail and build drawer, from another agent); bottom-left: slot `inspector`; bottom-centre: slot `word`. Pins and bubbles float over the canvas beneath all panels.
- Under 700px wide: the plaque becomes one condensed row across the top, heralds collapse to a bell seal with an unread count that opens a sheet, `inspector` and the build drawer become bottom sheets above the word bar, the word bar spans the width. Nothing scrolls sideways; minimum 16px gutters; safe-area insets respected.

**HUD plaque** (`hud.ts`): the realm's crest (`marginalia.crest`), realm name, the ruler's title in blackletter ("Reeve Luke"), the date line (`fmtDate`) with a small sun-and-moon dial showing `monthPhase` and a Rent Day countdown, speed seals (pause, 1x, 2x, 4x; the active one gold; shows "crawl" while crawling), and the four numbers with icons and tooltips that explain them: Coffers (with last month's net as a small signed figure; red in debt), Rent roll, Renown, Crown favour (meters), Households (with vacancies). Numbers tick smoothly when they change and flash gold or red. A charter progress pip row opens the charter screen. When `charter.ready`, a pulsing gold "The Crown's herald awaits" seal.

**Heralds** (`heralds.ts`): the feed as narrow parchment strips, newest on top, four or five visible, each with a kind icon and colour edge (`urgent` and `bad` rubric; `good` verdigris; `crown` gold; `rival` lapis; `staff`, `oath`, `info` ink), sliding in with a horn or chime, fading after a while; hovering holds them. Clicking focuses its building / opens its audience (`incidentId` whose def is an audience → `ctx.open('audience', incidentId)`). A "more" seal opens the full scrollable log with marginal doodles.

**Pins** (`pins.ts`): DOM markers anchored with `renderer.anchor`, updated every frame without layout thrash (transform only), scaled and faded by distance, clustered when they overlap, hidden when off-screen:
- incident pins on buildings: a wax-seal token with the def's icon, urgency colour and a timer ring draining to `expiresDay`. Click: audience incidents open the audience; others open a small parchment popover with the outcomes (default first, each showing its cost and who would go) which dispatches `resolve_incident`.
- purse pins (`unit.purse > 0`): a bobbing coin purse; click dispatches `collect_purse`. A "Collect all" appears on the HUD when two or more are out.
- applicant pins (waiting to be shown: click orders the showing; shown and awaiting a decision: opens the audience).
- petitioners waiting at the seat: a speech pin over the actor that opens the audience.
- construction and job progress rings; TO LET is the renderer's business.

**Bubbles** (`bubbles.ts`): short speech bubbles for `say` events, anchored to the actor / tenant / building, parchment with a tail, a typewriter reveal with `audio.voice` blips, at most three at once, queued. Also ambient barks: every so often pick a housed household whose figure is on screen and show a line from `BARKS` (src/data) fitting the moment (rent day, winter, a griffin nearby, unhappy, the ruler passing).

**Dev entry.** `src/dev/ui-shell.ts`: mount the UI over a flat dark background with a fake `Renderer` (implement the interface with no-ops and a simple fake `anchor` that spreads refs across the screen), `fakeSim(makeFixtureState('knight'))`, no-op word and the placeholder audio; push a few fake events.
