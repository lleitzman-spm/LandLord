# Brief: sim-decide

**Your files:** `src/sim/staff.ts`, `src/sim/incidents.ts`, `src/sim/effects.ts`, `src/sim/orders.ts`, `test/sim-decide.test.ts`.
**Read:** `src/sim/types.ts`, `core.ts`, `index.ts`, `src/data/schema.ts` (IncidentDef, Requires, Effect, OutcomeDef, TemperamentDef), `ids.ts`, `src/data/incidents/system.ts` and `examples.ts`, `balance.ts`, `ranks.ts`, `temperaments.ts`, `characters.ts` (STAFF_NAMES), and the other `src/sim/*.ts` files for the functions you call.

You own the heart of the game: everything that "comes up", and who settles it. ARCHITECTURE.md ("Incidents", "Deciding without the ruler") defines the behaviour; details:

**Incidents.** `spawnIncident`, `spawnDecision`, `resolveIncident` (validate: exists, open, outcome exists; refusals carry an in-world reason), `dismiss_incident`, timers and expiry, the random scheduler, the scripted first beats, cooldowns and `once` via `incidentLog`, delayed spawns via `pending` (the `spawn` effect). Target selection for the scheduler: for each candidate def find the targets its `requires` fit (`tenant`: a housed household; `building`: a ready building; `vacant`: an empty unit; `realm`: none) and pick one at random; skip defs with no target. Building-targeted incidents on an occupied building carry the occupant as `tenantId` so text and household effects work. Fill `vars` (core.baseVars plus `amount`, `staff`, `pet` as needed) once at spawn; herald and result text are filled from them. Audience incidents spawn a petitioner actor (tenant: from their door; applicant: their existing actor; npc: from the gate) walking to the seat's entrance and waiting; on close they walk home or out. If there is no room at court (3 already waiting) delay the spawn.
Caps on open incidents: 3 at Reeve, 5 at Knight, 7 at Baron. Urgent ones (urgency 3) may exceed by one.
Weather: you own `s.weather`. Roll it every few days by season (rain in spring and autumn, snow in winter, heat and storms in summer), emit `weather`.

**Effects.** Implement every `Effect` variant by calling the owning module. Household effects on a building-targeted incident apply to its occupants; scopes `building`, `neighbors` (radius 4), `all`. `gold` positive is `earn(windfall)`, negative is `spend(other)` unless the memo suggests bribes/court. `squatters_sign_lease` rolls a goblin household into the unit at 80% of asking with a signed lease. `oath` calls `oaths.swear` with the due day from `months`. Unknown or impossible effects are skipped silently, never thrown.

**Deciding.** `ordersFor` evaluates conditions against a context (season, month, gold, renown, cost of the default outcome's job, tenant fields with revealed traits only, building fields). `decide` as ARCHITECTURE specifies: `never` rules strike options; the most specific `choose` wins; then the domain delegate by authority and temperament `bias`; else null. When a delegate or an order settles something: resolve it, bump `order.uses`, emit `order_used`, mark `reported`, and send a `staff` herald in that delegate's voice saying what they did ("Agnes served notice on Wat Mudge, as is her habit."). A delegate acting on temperament should sometimes produce the comedy of delegation: a Grasping bailiff adds a fee the ruler did not ask for; a Snob crier turns away goblins. Keep it to their `bias` lists and `quirk`.
Handlers: `add_order` (validate shape, cap 40 orders, emit `order_added`), `revoke_order`, `set_policy` (clamp ranges), `resolve_incident`, `dismiss_incident`.

**Staff.** Candidates: three per unlocked role, rolled from `STAFF_NAMES` and `TEMPERAMENTS_BY_ROLE`, skill 1 to 4 weighted by renown, wage from `BAL.wageBySkill`, temperament hidden half the time. `hire` (needs the role unlocked, the staff building ready, the post empty; spawns their actor; herald), `dismiss_staff`, `set_wage` (loyalty reacts), `refresh_candidates` (small fee, monthly free refresh). Loyalty drifts with wage against skill, with how often their work is countermanded, and with broken oaths; at very low loyalty they quit with a parting line. Authority: 0 until the ruler is a Baron, then 1; 2 after a year of service at loyalty 70+. Skill grows slowly with jobs done.

**Tests** (`npx tsx test/sim-decide.test.ts`): every incident def in `INCIDENTS` can be spawned on a fitting target and every outcome resolved without a throw (build the fitting target by hand where needed); an order "never evict a widow in winter" strikes `evict`; temperament picks; expiry effects; the scheduler over three simulated years respects caps and cooldowns. Content files are filling up while you work: your engine must tolerate any valid def.
