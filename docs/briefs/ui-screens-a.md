# Brief: ui-screens-a (title, new reign, dynasty hall, end of reign, settings, help)

**Your files:** `src/ui/screens/title.ts`, `gameover.ts`, `settings.ts`, `help.ts`, `src/ui/css/screens-a.ts`, `src/dev/ui-screens-a.ts`.
**Read:** `src/ui/api.ts` (Screen, UICtx, AppAPI, DynastyEntry, Settings), `kit.ts`, `css/base.ts` (tokens and classes: use them), `icons.ts`, `marginalia.ts`, `portraits.ts` (art agents are filling these), `src/sim/types.ts`, `src/data/index.ts` (RANKS, REALM_NAMES), `src/dev/fixture.ts`. Each file exports `create(ctx): Screen`; call `addStyle` for your CSS inside `create`.

**Title** (`title.ts`): shown over the live table (the renderer is in `title` mode behind you: a slow orbit of a living hamlet), so it must not cover the view: the logo **LandLord** in blackletter with gold leaf and a rubric initial, a dry tagline ("A kingdom is a great many rents."), and a short column of manuscript buttons: Continue (when `app.hasSave()`, with the saved realm and rank), Begin a reign, Dynasty hall, Settings, How to rule. Margin doodles. A quiet footer line.
**Begin a reign**: a charter you fill in: your name, the realm's name (a "roll another" die that draws from `REALM_NAMES`), and the choice of mode as two facing parchment cards: **Steward's Peace** ("The moneylender is patient. A bad year is a setback.") and **Iron Crown** ("One reign. One save. When the coffers fail, so do you. Your heir inherits one heirloom."). Seal it to call `app.newGame`.
**Dynasty hall**: `app.dynasty()` as a wall of small memorial plaques (ruler, realm, rank reached, years, households, epitaph, heirloom), newest first; an empty hall has a wry line.
**End of reign** (`gameover.ts`, opened with the `EndState`): a full parchment: "Here ended the reign of …", the epitaph, the tallies (years, households housed, rent collected, evictions, oaths kept and broken, peak rent roll), the heirloom passed on (Iron Crown), and buttons: Begin anew, Dynasty hall, Title. Demotion in Steward's Peace reuses it with gentler wording and a "Carry on" button.
**Settings** (`settings.ts`): quality (low, medium, high), four volume sliders, "Wit of the court" (quick or considered: `wit`), hints on/off, the court key field (a password the server may require; stored via `app.setSettings`), Save now, Return to title. Applies live.
**Help** (`help.ts`, "How to rule"): a short illuminated primer in the game's voice, six or seven small sections with a doodle each: Rent Day; building and land; vacancies and applicants; when things break; ruling by word (with five or six example sayings a player can click to copy into the Word box: "Hob, mend the roof at the Leaning Hovel.", "Agnes, never evict a widow in winter.", "Three days' grace, then the fee."); audiences and oaths; the Crown and the charter. Also list the keys.

All screens: keyboard reachable, Esc closes, readable at 400px wide (stack; no sideways scroll), and beautiful: this is the first thing the owner sees.

**Dev entry.** `src/dev/ui-screens-a.ts`: mount each screen over a dark blurred stand-in ground with a fake `UICtx` and `AppAPI` (two fake dynasty entries, a fake save). Screenshot each at 1280×800 and 400×800; look; iterate.
