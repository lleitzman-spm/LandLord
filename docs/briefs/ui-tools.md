# Brief: ui-tools (the build book, the inspector, the Word bar)

**Your files:** `src/ui/parts/buildmenu.ts`, `inspector.ts`, `wordbar.ts`, `src/ui/css/tools.ts`, `src/dev/ui-tools.ts`.
**Read:** `src/ui/api.ts` (UICtx), `kit.ts`, `css/base.ts` (tokens and classes: use them), `parts/part.ts` (each file exports `mount(ctx): Part`; call `addStyle` for your CSS inside `mount`), `icons.ts`, `marginalia.ts`, `portraits.ts` (art agents are filling these), `src/render/api.ts` (Tool), `src/word/api.ts` (WordAPI, Exchange, MicAPI, SpeakTarget), `src/sim/types.ts`, `src/data/schema.ts`, `src/data/index.ts` (BUILDINGS, RANKS, TRAIT_DEFS, TRADE_DEFS, SPECIES_DEFS...), `src/dev/fixture.ts`.

Another agent owns the frame (`ui/index.ts`), which mounts your three parts into slots: `buildmenu` on the left edge under the HUD, `inspector` bottom-left, `wordbar` bottom-centre; under 700px they become bottom sheets and a full-width bar. Style only inside your own root elements.

**Build menu** (`buildmenu.ts`): a slim vertical **tool rail** of wax-seal buttons: Build (opens the pattern book), Road, Clear, Demolish, then Ledger, Council, Charter, Help (`ctx.open`). The **pattern book** is a parchment drawer: tabs Homes, Trades, Amenities, Household (staff buildings); each building a compact card with a small code-drawn pictogram (an SVG silhouette per type that you draw: recognisable, inked), name, cost, rent or aura, footprint, and its blurb on hover; locked ones (by `RANKS` up to the current rank) are greyed with "At Knight"; unaffordable ones show the cost in red. Choosing one calls `ctx.renderer.setTool({ kind: 'place', btype, rot })` and shows a small hint strip ("R to turn. Right-click to put it down."); R rotates; the tool stays armed for repeat placing until cancelled. Parcels for sale: a "Buy land" entry that highlights purchasable parcels (`renderer.setHighlight`) and lists them with names and prices (`buy_parcel`).

**Inspector** (`inspector.ts`): what is selected, as a compact parchment sheet.
- A building: name (click to rename), type, condition meter with a word for it ("Sound", "Shabby", "Held up by habit"), desirability if you can derive it, open incidents with their actions, units listed with who lives there (portrait, name, happiness face, rent, late flag), the **asking rent slider** with a market hint tick and a plain-words read ("A bargain. They will queue." / "Steep. Expect a wait."), list/unlist, and buttons: Repair, Renovate, Demolish, Visit. Under construction: progress. Vacant: who has applied.
- A household (click a tenant row or a townsperson): portrait, name, species and trade line, household size, tags, **traits** (revealed ones named with blurbs, hidden ones as "?" seals), happiness meter with the main reasons you can infer, lease (rent, months left, surety held), arrears and late stage in plain words, pets, **what they remember** (the memory list, newest first, with a small heart or thorn), oaths you swore them, and actions: Speak to them (focuses the Word bar addressed to them), Visit, Gift, Serve notice.
- A delegate: portrait, name, role, temperament (if known) with blurb, skill pips, wage (adjust), loyalty, current job, their standing orders, Speak, Dismiss.
- An applicant: portrait, who they are, what they will pay, Show the place / Hear them.
Everything updates live; nothing here pauses the world.

**Word bar** (`wordbar.ts`): the throne's voice. A text box ("Speak, {title}…") with a **microphone seal** (hold to talk, or hold the backquote key or Space when the box is empty and unfocused: check with the frame agent's hotkeys by only acting when no other field has focus). While listening or typing, `ctx.holdCrawl()`; partial transcripts appear live in the box. **Addressee chips**: To the court (default: the model routes it), each hired delegate by first name and role icon, and the selected household or building when there is one. Enter sends `ctx.word.speak(text, target)`. Then show the **exchange** just above the bar: the speaker's small portrait, their read-back typed out with `ctx.audio.voice` blips, and for `needs_confirm` two buttons, "Aye" and "Nay, I meant…" (which refocuses the box with the text restored). `refused` shows the reason in the speaker's voice; `unclear` invites a rephrase. Up-arrow recalls earlier sayings. A small scroll opens the log of recent exchanges. Show the transport state honestly and quietly: a lit candle icon when a model is answering, a dim one with the tip "No one at court can hear free speech. Plain commands and whispers still work." when offline. When the mic is unsupported or denied, hide the seal and note once that the keyboard's dictation key works in the box.
