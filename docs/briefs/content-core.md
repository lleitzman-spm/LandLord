# Brief: content-core (trades, peoples, traits, temperaments)

**Your files:** `src/data/trades.ts`, `species.ts`, `traits.ts`, `temperaments.ts`.
**Read:** `src/data/schema.ts` (TradeDef, SpeciesDef, TraitDef, TagDef, TemperamentDef), `ids.ts` (the id lists are frozen: you must cover every id, no more, no fewer), `buildings.ts` (which trades lease which premises), `ranks.ts`, `src/sim/types.ts` (DecisionType, DECISION_OPTIONS), `docs/STYLE.md` (species as tenants, delegate voices: these are your character sheets).

Replace the generated placeholders with real, hand-written data. Keep every export name and type. The sim reads the numbers; the model and the UI read the words, so both matter.

- **Trades** (all 28): name, typical monthly income (a laborer about 22, a farmer 28, craftsmen 35 to 50, a merchant or innkeeper 60 to 80, a wizard 110: rent should be a meaningful share of income), the premises they want (`shop`) where one exists in `BUILDINGS[*].trades`, `minRank`, and a blurb with a small joke about them as tenants.
- **Species** (all 6): names, adjective, `minRank` as in the placeholder, applicant `weight`, household range (goblins large, elves small), `traitBias`, what they `care` about in a home, a blurb, and **at least 45 first names and 45 surnames each**, invented, pronounceable and funny in the right register (halflings: Pimpernel Underbough; dwarves: Durga Caskhammer; goblins: Snik of the Good Pen, Grobnik Stamp-Twice; elves: Ffion Alderlight; ogres: one-word names like Grub, Mossback, Little Ern). No names from existing books, films or games.
- **Traits** (all 32) and **tags** (all 10): name, a revealed-blurb in the dry voice ("Quotes the Reeve's Charter from memory, and has a cousin at the court."), `good`, and sensible modifiers (`reliability` between −0.35 and +0.15, monthly `happiness` drift, `wear`, `patience`, `stay`). `ghost` is the tenant who is never home on Rent Day; `saint` is suspiciously perfect; `secret_pet` and `pyromaniac` feed incidents.
- **Temperaments** (all 16): name, epithet ("the Unyielding"), blurb, `bias` (ordered option preferences for every decision type in that role's domain: mason → `repair`; bailiff → `late_rent`, `eviction_ready`, `squatters`; crier → `applicant`; steward → `move_out_deposit`, `rent_renewal`, `pet`, and a view on `late_rent`), `speed` / `cost` / `quality` multipliers between 0.7 and 1.4 that make each a real trade-off, a `voice` paragraph precise enough for a model to imitate (diction, tics, what they care about, a sample sentence), **eight `acks`** (read-backs with `{order}` where the restated order goes, e.g. "{order}. It will hold. Probably.") and **six `grumbles`**, and a `quirk` (the thing they do when left to their own judgment).

Check: `node tools/check.mjs src/data` clean, and write a tiny throwaway script (do not commit it; put it in `.tmp/`) that asserts counts (names per species, acks per temperament) and that no string contains an em or en dash.
