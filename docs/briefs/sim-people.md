# Brief: sim-people

**Your files:** `src/sim/economy.ts`, `src/sim/tenants.ts`, `src/sim/rent.ts`, `src/sim/renown.ts`, `test/sim-people.test.ts`.
**Read:** `src/sim/types.ts`, `core.ts`, `index.ts`, `src/data/schema.ts`, `ids.ts`, `buildings.ts`, `balance.ts`, `ranks.ts`, `species.ts`, `trades.ts`, `traits.ts`, `characters.ts` (content is being filled in by others; code against the schema), and the other `src/sim/*.ts` files for the functions you call.

Replace the placeholder bodies. ARCHITECTURE.md ("Rent Day", ownership table) defines your behaviour; details:

**Economy.** `earn` / `spend` update `gold`, the month book (by category) and the capped transaction log (pass tenant/building refs through to the Txn and the `gold` event). `onMonth`: close the last book, open the next, charge wages (sum of staff wages) and upkeep (ready buildings), track `stats.peakRentRoll`. `marketRent(b)`: `baseRent × (0.6 + 0.8 × desirability/100) × (0.7 + 0.3 × condition/100)`, nudged up a little as the realm grows, whole crowns. `set_rent` sets the asking rent (1 to 5× base) for new leases only. `gift` (category `gifts`, happiness by size, a fond memory). `hold_feast` (`BAL.feastCost`, everyone +8 happiness, renown +3, once per six months, herald, `sfx cheer`).

**Households.** `rollHousehold`: species weighted among `rank.speciesUnlocked` and the building's `prefers` / `only`; a trade that suits (commercial units take only `def.trades`); names from `SPECIES_DEFS`; one to three hidden traits with species bias; tags; size; income from the trade with variance; reliability from 0.78 plus trait modifiers; pets for pet lovers. Use a fitting named `CHARACTERS` entry about a third of the time (never twice in one realm). **Init:** two starting households in two of the three hovels with leases and deposits.

**Applicants.** Each day each listed vacancy may draw an applicant: `BAL.applicantRate` per month, scaled by renown, by desirability against asking rent (over market: fewer; a bargain: more), by a Crier's skill, by a notice board. One applicant per unit at a time. They appear at `actors.gatePoint`, walk to the door, and wait `BAL.applicantWaitDays`. If a Crier exists a `show` job is queued for them; otherwise the ruler must be sent (`order_job`). After the showing: `incidents.spawnDecision(s, 'applicant', { applicantId, buildingId, unit })`. `applyLease` signs them (deposit = `policies.depositMonths` × rent into the coffers as held surety; they refuse above `maxRent` and walk) or turns them away (they leave by the gate; small renown cost if they waited long). `screen` reveals hidden traits.

**Mood.** Monthly, happiness moves a third of the way toward a target built from: rent against market, building condition, desirability, open incidents at their door, traits, season (a cold winter in a poor house), fresh memories. Below 25 for two months they give notice and go (`unhappy`). Each month a small chance to reveal one hidden trait with a herald line. A `visit` job from the ruler lifts happiness and may reveal a trait. Keep departed households 60 days, then prune.

**Rent Day and the late pipeline.** As ARCHITECTURE describes: payers over days 1 to 3; purses (or direct payment with Steward + Counting House); Bailiff auto-collects; stale purses become arrears without fault. Non-payers after `graceDays`: `spawnDecision('late_rent')`. `applyLateOption`: `wait` (a week more, then ask again), `late_fee` (`lateFeePct` of rent onto arrears; they usually pay), `payment_plan` (arrears over three months; reliability up), `forgive` (clear; a little realm-wide reliability drift as word spreads), `notice` (court clock `BAL.courtDays`, then `spawnDecision('eviction_ready')`), `cash_for_keys` (pay them, they leave today), `evict` (queue the Bailiff; `evict()` on completion: `moveOut('evicted')`, renown, neighbours' mood, stats), `mercy` (clear the case, keep the arrears on a plan). Arrears are paid down first whenever they pay.

**Leases and leaving.** At term end `spawnDecision('rent_renewal')`; `changeLeaseRent` applies the percent with reactions (and respects `renewalCapPct` by clamping). On departure with a surety held: an `inspect` job, damage = their `wear` applied to the building, then `spawnDecision('move_out_deposit')`; `applyDeposit(keepPct)` books `deposits_kept` / `deposits_returned` (−1 = exactly the damage cost). Unlisted or long-empty units (40+ days) risk `spawnDecision('squatters', { buildingId, unit })`.

**Renown and favor.** `changeRenown` / `changeFavor` clamp and emit. Monthly renown drifts toward a blend of 50 and average happiness (`BAL.renownDrift`).
